Contact us

Have a question about a deal, a booking, or your membership? Here's the fastest way to reach the right place.

General questions

For anything about the site, our deals, or how member pricing works, email us at support@cheapgetaway.com and we'll point you in the right direction.

Booking help

Booking confirmations, changes, and cancellations are handled directly with our booking provider, who processes and fulfills every reservation made through cheapgetaway.com. If you need help with an existing booking, please have your confirmation number ready — you'll find it in your booking confirmation email — and reach out to the booking provider's support team listed there for the fastest resolution. If you're not sure where to start, email support@cheapgetaway.com with your confirmation number and we'll help route you.

Privacy requests

To access, correct, delete, export, or restrict use of your personal information, email support@cheapgetaway.com with the subject line "Privacy request." See our Privacy Policy for details on your rights.

Response time

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 2 business days.
