About us

About cheapgetaway.com

Most travel sites show you the same public rate everyone else sees. We go find the rate that isn't public — the member-level pricing normally reserved for travel agents, corporate accounts, and loyalty tiers — and put it in front of you before you book.

What we do

Our search connects to travel data partners with access to 2+ million properties worldwide, so instead of guessing which site has the best deal on a given hotel, you see live, member-tier pricing side by side with the standard rate in one search. We also run free webinars that walk through exactly how member pricing works and how to use it on your own future trips — the goal is for you to leave knowing more about how hotel pricing actually works, not just to book one trip.

How we make money

We're upfront about this because it's the honest thing to do and because you deserve to know who's paying whom. The rates you see come from licensed travel wholesale partners. When you book a stay through cheapgetaway.com, the booking itself — payment, confirmation, cancellations — is fulfilled by our third-party booking provider, and we may earn a commission on that booking. If you join the travel members' club through one of our webinars, that enrollment may also compensate us. None of this changes the price you pay: commissions come out of what our partners already pay for the referral, not out of your wallet.

What we are — and what we're not

  • We're a deals-discovery and travel-education site. We're not a hotel, an airline, or a licensed travel agency.
  • We don't hold your payment, issue your confirmation, or run your cancellation — that's handled by our booking provider, using the same rails as much of the travel industry.
  • We do the work of finding and explaining better pricing so you don't have to dig through a dozen tabs to find it yourself.

What we care about

  • Real prices. Every rate on this site is checked live against our data partners' inventory — nothing is invented or padded to make a "discount" look bigger.
  • No invented urgency. If we show you a limited-time price, it's because the underlying rate is actually time-limited, not because a countdown timer converts better.
  • Your data stays yours. We don't sell your personal information. Read the specifics in our Privacy Policy.
