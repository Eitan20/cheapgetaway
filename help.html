Help center

Answers to the questions we hear most. If you don't see yours below, reach out and a real person will get back to you.

How does member-level pricing work?

The rates you see side by side with the standard price come from licensed travel wholesale partners — the same kind of member-tier pricing normally reserved for travel agents and corporate accounts. We surface it live, right next to the standard rate, so you can compare before you book.

Where's my booking confirmation?

Confirmations are sent by email from our booking provider, who processes every reservation made through cheapgetaway.com. Check your spam or promotions folder first. If it still hasn't turned up, contact us with your confirmation number and we'll help track it down.

How do I change or cancel a booking?

Changes and cancellations are handled directly by the booking provider — use the links or contact details in your confirmation email to manage your reservation. Cancellation terms vary by rate, and some rates are non-refundable; that's always shown clearly before you book.

When will I get my refund?

Refund timing depends on the supplier and your payment processor. Once a refund is confirmed, it typically takes 5–10 business days to appear back on your card or account.

What is the club?

It's a free webinar that walks through how member pricing works and how the travel members' club can save you money on future trips. No cost to attend — you can join via our webinar signup.

I have a question that isn't listed

Reach out through our contact page or email support@cheapgetaway.com. We respond within 2 business days.
