Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how cheapgetaway.com ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you browse the site, search for trips, sign in, book a stay, or register for a webinar.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details you provide, such as name and email, when you sign in, create an account, book a stay, or register for a webinar.
  • Search and booking details you enter, such as destinations, dates, and traveler counts, used to fetch live availability from our travel data partners.
  • Basic usage data (pages viewed, browser type) collected automatically to keep the site working well.

2. How we use information

  • To show live hotel availability and pricing.
  • To confirm webinar registrations and send you the join link for your session.
  • To manage your account and any saved trips.
  • To improve the site and prevent misuse.

3. Sharing

We share booking search details with the travel data providers necessary to return live rates and complete a reservation. When you book, your booking and payment details are processed by our third-party booking provider and its payment processors — we do not store your full payment card number. We do not sell your personal information. Webinar registration details may be shared with the webinar and membership platforms (including Travorium) solely to deliver the session and enrollment experience you signed up for.

4. Cookies and local storage

We use cookies and browser local storage to keep you signed in, remember webinar registration details for the current session, and understand how the site is used.

5. Data retention

We keep account and booking information for as long as needed to provide the Services and meet legal obligations, then delete or anonymize it.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information. That said, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

The Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. You must be at least 18 years old to make a booking through the Services.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under applicable law — such as the EU/UK GDPR or California's CCPA/CPRA — to access, correct, delete, export, or restrict our processing of your personal information, and to opt out of marketing communications. Transactional messages, such as booking confirmations and webinar session details, will continue even if you opt out of marketing. You can exercise these rights by reaching out through our contact page.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy, or requests to exercise your privacy rights, can be sent through our contact page.

This page provides a general placeholder privacy policy for a travel-membership marketing site and does not constitute legal advice.
